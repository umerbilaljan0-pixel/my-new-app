Marketplaces reward consistency. A grid of products, each cleanly cut out on the
same background, reads as a real shop. Mismatched, cluttered shots read as risk.
Here's how to get there quickly.

## Step 1 — Cut out the subject

Open [Cut Out](/remove-background) and drop your product photo. One pass isolates
the subject and removes the background, keeping a true alpha channel so edges stay
soft instead of jagged.

## Step 2 — Pick your background

- **Transparent** — best if you'll place the product on different coloured pages.
- **Solid white** — the safe default for most marketplaces.
- **Studio grey** — a softer look that still reads as "clean".

## Step 3 — Keep it consistent

Shoot on a plain, evenly-lit background where you can. Cut Out is tuned for
exactly this case, and a clean input gives a clean edge. Then apply the same
background and the same export size to every product so your grid lines up.

## Step 4 — Export at the right size

Free downloads are 1200px on the long edge — fine for most listings. For print or
a hero banner, grab full resolution, or send the cut-out straight into
[Upscale](/upscale-image) to enlarge it to 2K or 4K without re-uploading.

## Common mistakes

- **Busy backgrounds.** A product on a patterned rug is harder to isolate cleanly
  than one on a plain sheet.
- **Motion blur.** Soft edges in the source become soft edges in the cut-out.
- **Over-compression.** Start from the best copy you have, not a re-saved JPG.
